Your Points, Decoded

You hold Delta SkyMiles and Amex Membership Rewards. Amex transfers 1:1 to Delta and to several partners that often price the same flights for fewer miles. Always check these before booking with SkyMiles directly:

ProgramTransferWhy it matters for Europe
Flying Blue (Air France/KLM)Amex 1:1SkyTeam like Delta — books Delta, AF & KLM flights, often far cheaper than SkyMiles for the same seat. Monthly "Promo Rewards" discount specific routes 25–50%.
Virgin AtlanticAmex 1:1Can book Delta-operated flights, sometimes at a fraction of Delta's own price — the classic Delta One sweet spot.
Aeroplan (Air Canada)Amex 1:1Star Alliance: Lufthansa, Swiss, Austrian, TAP, SAS — great for Christmas-market cities (Munich, Vienna, Zurich). Allows a stopover for 5k miles.
British Airways / Iberia AviosAmex 1:1Iberia to Madrid and Aer Lingus to Dublin are cheap in Avios with low surcharges. Avoid BA through London (high fees).
Delta SkyMilesAmex 1:1 (you also earn these directly)No award chart — pricing floats with cash price. Best deals appear in flash sales and on off-peak dates; the calendar view on delta.com is your friend.

Rules of thumb

  • Never transfer Amex speculatively. Find the award space first, confirm the price, then transfer (usually instant to all of the above).
  • Price the same seat 3 ways: Delta miles, Flying Blue, Virgin Atlantic. It's routinely a 2–3× spread.
  • Lap infant on international awards: Delta charges 10% of the adult cash fare in dollars; some partners charge 10% in miles. Award tickets with a lap infant often must be ticketed by phone — book the adults online, then call.
  • One-stop trick: the expensive part is crossing the Atlantic. If Sardinia (or anywhere secondary) is pricey, book the great transatlantic deal to a hub (Milan, Paris, Amsterdam) and buy the short hop separately — overnight near the hub if the connection is tight.
  • December pattern: Dec 24–26 and Jan 1–4 departures are frequently the cheapest of the entire month; Dec 19–22 outbound is peak.
